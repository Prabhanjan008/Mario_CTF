🚨 Incident Briefing: Operation Recover The Golden Star

At 03:14:07 AM, the Mushroom Kingdom Master Sanctum vault perimeter collapsed. The kingdom’s core power source, THE GOLDEN STAR, was exfiltrated by rogue syndicate 'PHANTOM_SHROOM'. As cyber operatives, your mission is to investigate compromised sectors on the 3D World Map, extract forensic clues, defeat Bowser’s mainframe shields, and recover the Golden Star.

🎮 2. Scoring System & Game Economy

🏆 Forensic Points (Score)

Every case cleared awards between 100 to 500 PTS based on difficulty. Solves are atomically registered on the blockchain-style immutable ledger.

⭐ Golden Stars (0 - 12)

Cases 1–11 award 1 Star each. Accumulate stars to dissolve quantum firewalls guarding higher sectors on the 3D map. Clear all 11 to breach the Boss Fortress.

🪙 Cyber Coins & 3D ? Blocks

Each squad starts with 100 Cyber Coins. Earn more by solving cases or clicking floating 3D Question Blocks on the world map. Spend coins to decrypt hints!

⏱️ Monotonic Competition Timer

High-precision timer synchronized with the server's UTC ledger. Automatically freezes the exact millisecond the final flag is verified or upon squad conclusion.

👥 3. Multi-Member Squad Collaboration

  • Squad Access Code: The lead operative registers the squad name and receives a unique cryptographic access code (e.g., CYBER-W7KP-92RM).
  • Live Team Concurrency: Teammates join from their own laptops by entering the access code and their name.
  • Shared State Synchronization: All squad members share live forensic solves, star progress, coins, and hint logs without conflicting state or race conditions.

📜 4. Official Tournament Rules

  • Flag Submission Format: All flags strictly adhere to the format FLAG{SAMPLE_FLAG_TEXT}. Flags are case-sensitive and must include the curly braces.
  • Anti-Brute-Force Rate Limiting: Automated script-based brute forcing against challenge submission endpoints is restricted to a maximum of 10 attempts per 30 seconds per squad.
  • Hint Transparency: Purchasing hints deducts Cyber Coins permanently from your squad's balance. Hints are progressive (Hint 1 → Hint 2).
  • Deterministic Tie-Breaking Protocol: Ties on the official leaderboard are resolved in strict deterministic order:
    1. 1. Total Forensic Score (PTS) DESC
    2. 2. Normal Golden Stars Recovered DESC
    3. 3. Mission Completion Status (Boss Fortress cleared first)
    4. 4. Total Elapsed Mission Time ASC
    5. 5. Timestamp of Final Flag Solve ASC

⚠️ Prohibited Actions (Immediate Disqualification & Removal)

  • Infrastructure Tampering: Do NOT launch Denial-of-Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, automated fuzzing scanners, or exploit attempts against the host server or platform APIs. All investigations are strictly self-contained within challenge workbenches.
  • Cross-Squad Collusion: Sharing flags, keys, solutions, or access codes with other competing squads is strictly prohibited.
  • Account Tampering: Do NOT attempt to hijack or brute force other squads' access codes or sessions.

🚀 5. Operative Quickstart Checklist

Step 1: Navigate to the game portal at the announced tournament link and click "REGISTER SQUAD".
Step 2: Enter your squad name and operative handle. Copy and securely share your Squad Access Code with your teammates.
Step 3: Review the Mission Briefing and inspect Sector 01 on the 3D World Map.
Step 4: Click on Case nodes to open forensic terminals, examine evidence, crack payloads, and submit flags.
Step 5: Recover all 11 Golden Stars, breach Bowser’s Fortress in Sector 05, and lock in your final time!
